**Ghrita's Place in Tradition and Food**

Ayurveda calls ghee ghrita and uses it in food traditions as well as prepared formulations. Sweetness, a cooling character and an unctuous quality are traditional descriptions within that system. They tell us how Ayurveda understands the ingredient, not what a modern nutritional test has measured.
Remember that distinction when you encounter wide health claims about A2 Desi ghee. A traditional idea of nourishment does not prove that a food prevents illness or improves a specific bodily function. A long-standing kitchen habit can have value without every claimed outcome being established.
For daily meals, look first at what you can identify: the food itself, its preparation and the dish receiving it. Respect the traditional setting without assuming an Ayurvedic term promises the same result for every person.
